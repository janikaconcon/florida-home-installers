About us

A local, women-owned installer that answers the phone

Florida Home Installers is a self-owned, women-owned installation company based in Lakeland, Florida. We were founded on a simple idea: homeowners deserve a contractor who shows up, explains the work in plain language, and stands behind the finished job.

Owner Danielle DiDonato is personally involved in every project. Customers repeatedly mention that she returns calls, replies to emails within a day, and stays reachable from the first measurement to the final walkthrough — a level of accountability that is rare in this trade.
